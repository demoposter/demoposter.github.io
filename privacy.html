Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. What we collect

When an authorized user connects a social media account, we receive an access token and basic profile information (such as account ID, username and display name) from that platform. We also store the content we choose to publish (text, images, videos).

2. How we use it

We use this information only to publish our own content to the connected account and to check the result of each post. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or selling data.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, except with the platform itself when publishing (for example, sending a video to TikTok).

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored in our private automation system and are only accessible to the owner. Tokens expire or can be revoked at any time.

5. Children

The Service is not directed to children and is operated only by its adult owner.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect the Service at any time in the app settings of each platform. To ask us to delete stored data, email us.
